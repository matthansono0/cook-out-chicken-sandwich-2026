Cook Out Chicken Sandwich: Grilled, Crispy, Cajun, and Club Styles Explained

By Matt Hanson, November 23, 2026

Cook Out is famous for its trays, burgers, and milkshakes, but the chicken sandwich deserves more attention than it gets. It comes in several styles, it costs less than most fast food chicken sandwiches, and it works just as well on its own as it does as the main item on a tray. Here is how the Cook Out chicken sandwich lineup works, which style to order, and how to get the best value from it.

The styles on the menu

The most talked about version is the Cajun Chicken Sandwich. It is a spicy fried chicken sandwich, and it is the one regulars recommend first. On a recent Raleigh menu board it was listed at $2.19 with 520 calories, which makes it one of the cheapest spicy chicken sandwiches from any national chain. The heat is real but not punishing, and it comes with the standard toppings, so it is a good first order if you have never tried a Cook Out chicken sandwich before.

The grilled chicken sandwich is the lighter option. It swaps the fried coating for a grilled fillet, which keeps the sandwich satisfying without the extra crunch and grease. If you are watching calories or you just prefer grilled chicken, this is the version to ask for. The crispy chicken sandwich sits between the two: a classic fried fillet with a plain, crunchy coating and no Cajun spice, which suits anyone who wants fried chicken without the heat.

The chicken club is the loaded version. It builds on the chicken sandwich with bacon, cheese, and the usual toppings, and it is the best choice when you want a sandwich that eats like a full meal. Cook Out also serves chicken strips, which are a different item, but they share the same kitchen logic: simple, affordable chicken, cooked to order.

How it fits on a tray

A chicken sandwich on its own is cheap, but on a tray it becomes a full meal. A regular Cook Out tray pairs your main item with two sides and a drink, and a junior tray is a smaller version of the same idea. Recent menu boards listed regular trays around $7.99 and junior trays around $6.99, though prices vary by location, so check the board at your restaurant. On a tray, the chicken sandwich is a strong pick because the sides do the heavy lifting: fries, onion rings, hushpuppies, or a second side of your choice turn a $2 sandwich into a filling dinner for under $8.

The other tray trick is the milkshake upgrade. Cook Out lets you swap the tray drink for a fancy milkshake, and the upgrade runs $1.00 to $1.60 depending on the location and the flavor. With more than 40 milkshake flavors on the menu, pairing a spicy Cajun chicken sandwich with a cool vanilla or Oreo shake is one of the best value combinations in fast food. The contrast between the spicy sandwich and the cold shake is exactly why regulars order it that way.

What to order first

If it is your first time, order the Cajun Chicken Sandwich as a regular tray with fries and a Cheerwine or sweet tea. That order shows off everything Cook Out does well: a cheap, spicy main, two solid sides, and a Southern drink, all for tray money. If you like heat, add a second Cajun sandwich on the side rather than a second tray; at around $2 each, the sandwich alone is the cheapest way to add more food to your order.

If you do not like spice, order the crispy chicken sandwich the same way. It gives you the same value and the same tray structure without the Cajun heat. And if you want the most food per dollar, the chicken club tray is the pick: the bacon and cheese make it the most filling chicken sandwich on the menu, and it still costs less than a combo at most burger chains.

A few things worth knowing

Prices vary by location, so the figures in this guide are a snapshot from recent menu boards, not a promise for every restaurant. Cook Out does not publish one national price list, which is why I track prices location by location. For current tray prices and the full chicken lineup, the Cook Out Cajun chicken guide at CookoutsMenue.com has the latest numbers I have verified, at https://cookoutsmenue.com/cookout-cajun-chicken/.

The chicken sandwich also works as a late night order. Many Cook Out locations stay open late, and the drive thru is built for quick orders, so a Cajun chicken sandwich and a shake is an easy late night stop. Just remember that the menu can vary slightly by location, and some smaller restaurants may not carry every style, so it is worth checking the board before you commit to a specific sandwich.

The bottom line is simple: the Cook Out chicken sandwich is one of the best value items on the menu. The Cajun version is spicy, cheap, and widely loved, the grilled version is the lighter choice, and the club is the most filling. Put any of them on a tray with two sides and a shake upgrade, and you have a full meal for less than a combo anywhere else.
